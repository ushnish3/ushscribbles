What does the pricing include?

  • Understanding the requirements and learning about your business, audience, products, brand, and approach.
  • Sending and receiving content briefing emails and notes (free fillable notes on request).
  • Proactive communication asking for details about your requirements.
  • Adhering to provided guidelines (Oxford comma, headline formatting, hyphens).
  • SEO-optimized writing following best practices.
  • Writing original, unique, and well-researched content.
  • Natural keyword and semantic insertion.
  • External linking to non-competitive websites or your website.
  • Developing authority through deep research.
  • Using personal experience and technical expertise.
  • Content formatting (headings, short paragraphs, scannable layout).
  • Providing relevant examples and analogies.
  • Communication, initial consultation, and Q&A.
  • Two rounds of revisions.
  • Timely delivery on a reasonable schedule.
  • Confirmation emails, submission emails, and invoicing.

What the pricing doesn't include?

  • Product/Service Testing: Trying out products or services to write about them (e.g., testing CRM functionality) is charged at an hourly rate.
  • Team Meetings: Initial consultation is free. If your team needs me to join ongoing meetings, hourly charges apply.
  • Media Consumption: Listening to or watching interviews, video content, and podcasts for research is charged on a per-hour basis.
  • CMS Uploads: Content management services like WordPress formatting/uploads are invoiced per hour.
